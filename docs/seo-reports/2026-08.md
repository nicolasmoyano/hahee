# SEO Report — 2026-08

Reporting period: 1–31 August 2026. Comparison: 1–31 July 2026. GA4 property: `514293418` (Europe/Stockholm).

> **Sample-size note:** August had 58 sessions and July had 14. Percent changes are therefore volatile. `booking_initiated` is a booking-link click, not a confirmed appointment. Page-level session counts are non-additive because one session can visit several pages.

## Executive Summary

- Sessions rose from 14 to 58 (+314%), while Google organic sessions rose from 7 to 12 (+71%). Including Google Business Profile (`gbp / organic`), organic traffic was 14 sessions.
- Booking-link clicks rose from 3 to 8, but the session-level click rate fell from 21.4% to 13.8% (-7.6 percentage points). The volume is too small to infer a durable conversion trend.
- The homepage produced 4 booking clicks. `/huvudvark` produced 2 from only 3 page-associated sessions; this is promising but too small for a conclusion. `/naprapat-odenplan` had 16 page-associated sessions, 68.8% bounce rate and 1 booking click.
- GA4 traffic attribution was being polluted by the booking event parameter named `source`: CTA labels such as `hero_home` and `naprapat_odenplan_cta` appeared as session sources. This report's P1 code change renames it to `cta_location`.
- No P0 outage was found: the homepage, key landing pages and ClinicBuddy booking URL returned HTTP 200 on 1 September.

Reassess after a clean month of attribution data and Search Console impressions.

- Exclude internal/test traffic where practical and investigate `vercel.com / referral` if it persists, so acquisition reports better reflect prospective patients.
- After deployment, register `cta_location` as an event-scoped custom dimension in GA4 if CTA-placement reporting is needed in the GA interface.
- Watch zero/near-zero symptom pages (`/ischias`, `/nacksmarta`, `/idrottsskador`) in Search Console before deciding on content changes; strengthen pages only where impressions indicate demand.
