# SEO Report — 2026-09

Reporting period: 1–30 September 2026. Comparison: 1–31 August 2026. GA4 property: `514293418` (Europe/Stockholm). Search Console property: `sc-domain:naprapat-hanna.se`.

> **Sample-size note:** September had 42 sessions and 10 booking-link clicks. Percent changes are volatile at this volume. `booking_initiated` measures clicks to ClinicBuddy, not confirmed appointments. Page-level session counts are non-additive because one session can visit several pages.

## Executive Summary

- Sessions fell from 58 to 42 (-27.6%), users from 48 to 33 (-31.2%), and page views from 93 to 51 (-45.2%). Bounce rate improved from 62.1% to 54.8%, but average session duration fell from 2m 20s to 1m 01s.
- Booking-link clicks rose from 8 to 10 (+25.0%). The directional clicks-per-session rate rose from 13.8% to 23.8% (+10.0 percentage points); nine of the ten clicks came from the homepage and one from `/priser`.
- GA4 organic sessions increased from 14 to 21 (+50.0%), including Google, GBP and Bing. Search Console moved in the opposite direction: clicks fell from 18 to 13 (-27.8%) and impressions from 544 to 365 (-32.9%). The systems use different attribution and counting rules, so neither should be substituted for the other.
- Search Console shows `/naprapat-odenplan` as “Discovered – currently not indexed”. The homepage, not the local landing page, received 31 impressions for `naprapat odenplan` at average position 29.9. `/priser` was unknown to Google and missing from the sitemap.
- The site-side `source` → `cta_location` fix was deployed on 12 September, but the published GTM container still maps a legacy `source` parameter in the `booking_initiated` tag. Six September sessions were still attributed to CTA labels. GTM must be updated and republished.
